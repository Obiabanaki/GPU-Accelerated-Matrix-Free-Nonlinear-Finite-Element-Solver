Detailed Description

Incomplete-LU preconditioner wrapping Eigen::IncompleteLUT.

Step 2.2. CPU-only by construction (needs the full matrix to factor, not just applyTo) — see ARCHITECTURE.md §7 for the two options (widen LinearOperator vs. scope Jacobi/ILU to CPU) and pick one before Step 3.3.
